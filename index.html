I am a PhD candidate in computer science at Université Laval, advised by Prof. Jean-François Lalonde in the Computer Vision & Systems Lab. My research focuses on understanding, representing, and manipulating lighting with generative models.

I interned at Adobe Research in London in 2025 and 2026, working on lighting representations and image relighting with Valentin Deschaintre, Iliyan Georgiev, Michael Fischer, Yannick Hold-Geoffroy, Sam Sartor, and Zhixin Shu. My follow-up work explores the editability and controllability of lighting representations.

Previously, I completed my MEng in Computer Technologies at South China University of Technology with Prof. Chuhua Xian, studying consistent depth estimation and BRDF estimation. I also interned as an Unreal Engine developer at Alibaba Lingxi Interactive and as a rendering developer at Revobit, a cloud rendering start-up for the fashion industry. I received my bachelor's degree in E-Commerce from Xidian University.

I expect to graduate in early/mid 2027 and am looking for full-time research positions.
